From exact geometry
directly to simulation

ADIGA connects design and analysis through a unified geometric representation, reducing geometry cleanup, repeated conversion and model reconstruction. High-order splines, embedded isogeometric analysis and intelligent simulation form a new engineering computation foundation.

01 / STRUCTURES

ADIGA / Struct

Static, dynamic and complex structural analysis with unified management of geometry, materials, boundaries and solvers.

02 / FLUIDS

ADIGA / Fluid

Isogeometric and embedded-domain computation for complex flows and high-fidelity fluid simulation.

03 / EXPLICIT

ADIGA / Explicit

Explicit dynamics workflows for impact, crashworthiness, large deformation and strongly nonlinear problems.

04 / INTELLIGENCE

ADIGA / SimAI

Physics, simulation data and AI for rapid prediction, design optimization and agent-driven simulation.

INDUSTRIALIZATION

From original theory
to engineering impact

ADIGA grew from research on embedded-domain isogeometric CAE theory, computation and software. It was selected for an industrialization pilot program of China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology for high-tech achievements from the National Key R&D Program.
