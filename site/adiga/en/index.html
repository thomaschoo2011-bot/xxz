ONE GEOMETRY, ONE WORKFLOW

Make the geometry model the analysis model

ADIGA unifies exact CAD geometry, spline representations and high-fidelity computation, reducing repeated conversion, repair and reconstruction between CAD and CAE.

01 · GEOMETRY

Exact geometry

A unified representation built around B-Rep, NURBS and B++ splines.

02 · ANALYSIS

Isogeometric computation

Spline bases connect geometric representation and numerical analysis.

03 · ENGINEERING

Engineering workflow

Materials, boundaries, solvers, post-processing and intelligent simulation.

RECOGNITION

From national R&D prototype to industrialization

ADIGA grew from research on embedded-domain isogeometric CAE theory, computational methods and software implementation.

ADIGA was selected for an industrialization pilot program of China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology for high-tech achievements originating from the National Key R&D Program.
